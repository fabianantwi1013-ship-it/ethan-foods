Our story

From grandma's kitchen to your fridge.

Natural drinks that nourish the mind, body and soul — combining super fruits and roots into an authentic, healthy drink.

Why we exist

Age-old roots. A brand-new bottle.

The desire for all-natural teas and juices with age-old roots, spices and fruits — like ginger, moringa, turmeric, hibiscus and sea moss — is why Ethan Foods was born.

Our recipes were handed down by grandma, who made these drinks for special occasions and sold them at the local market. What started as a family tradition is now bottled with the same care — nothing artificial, nothing rushed.

We manufacture in small batches to preserve the nutritional value of every root and fruit that goes into the bottle — including our specialty moringa tea blends.
