Our story

A recipe handed down by grandma.

The desire for all-natural teas and juices with age-old roots, spices and fruits — ginger, moringa, turmeric, hibiscus and sea moss — is why Ethan Foods was born.

"Natural drinks that nourish the mind, body and soul — combining super fruits and roots into an authentic, healthy drink."

Grandma made these drinks for special occasions and sold them at the market. Today we bottle them in small batches to preserve every bit of their nutritional value.
